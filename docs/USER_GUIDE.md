## 1. What This App Does

Goal Planning App helps you organize your money by hand. You enter your income, accounts, bills, cards, loans and spending. The app works out:

- how your month is going,
- what you still have to pay,
- how much you owe,
- what money you have,
- what you are saving for.

**You stay in control.** The app never changes a goal, a payment, a date or an amount unless you choose to and confirm it.

**What it doesn't do:**

- It doesn't connect to your bank. Nothing is imported automatically.
- It doesn't move real money. Marking a bill as paid only records that you paid it.
- It doesn't contact any company. Pausing or cancelling a subscription in the app only records your plan.
- It doesn't give financial advice. Estimates and simulations show what could happen, not what you should do.

## 2. Where Your Information Is Kept

- Everything you enter is saved **in this browser, on this device only**.
- It is **not encrypted** and **not sent anywhere**. Anyone who can use this browser can see it.
- Another browser, another device or a private window won't see it.
- If you clear this site's data in your browser settings, your information is gone.
- **Download a Backup regularly** and keep the file somewhere safe. See [Reports and Backups](#17-reports-and-backups).
- Hiding amounts only changes what you see on screen. It doesn't protect what is saved.

## 3. Your Space

Your space holds all your information. You create it once.

1. On the Welcome screen, choose **Create Account** or **Log In**. Both are previews: no account is created and no password is stored.
2. Choose **Continue Without an Account**.
3. Complete **Welcome Setup**:
   - **Your Profile**: how you appear in your space.
   - **Your Plan**: who will use the space. You can only choose this now.
     - **Individual**: your own money and goals.
     - **Duo**: works on this device only. Everyone who uses it can see everything in the space.
     - **Group**: coming soon.
   - **Your Preferences**: currency, language and appearance. The currency can't be changed later, and amounts are never converted. English is the only language for now.
   - **Privacy & Terms**: what this version does with your information.
4. Choose **Create My Space**. Your space starts empty: the app never adds income, accounts, loans or goals for you.

In a Duo space, Home's third area is called **Our Money & Goals** instead of Money & Goals.

**Settings** (the gear at the top) has:

- **Account & Workspace**: your space, its type and its members.
- **Preferences**: appearance (System, Light or Dark) and language. Currency is shown but can't be changed.
- **Privacy & Security**: **Erase All Data on This Device**. This can't be undone. Download a Backup first.
- **Reports & Backup**: the PDF report, **Download Backup** and **Restore Backup**. See [Reports and Backups](#17-reports-and-backups).
- **Explore the Demo**: see [The Demo](#4-the-demo).

## 4. The Demo

The demo shows a full invented year (2026) for a person called Alex. The demo's date is **31 December 2026**, so "today" in the demo means that date.

**Opening it**

- From the Welcome screen: **Explore the App**.
- From your own space: **Settings › Explore the Demo**.
  - If you typed something that isn't saved yet, the app asks first. You can choose **Copy What I Typed**, **Keep Editing** or **Discard and Open Demo**.
  - While your last change is still being saved, the demo doesn't open ("Wait until your last change is saved.").

**While it's open**

A strip at the top says **Demo · Invented data**. It has:

- **Exit Demo**, always visible.
- **Demo options** (the ⋯ button). It explains that everything is invented and that changes aren't saved, and it has **Reset Demo**.

**Reset Demo** asks first: **Reset demo?** with **Cancel** and **Reset Demo**. It puts back the whole example: Alex's records, the Home layout, the views and Alex's screen preferences. Reset Demo is also in Settings while the demo is open.

**Nothing in the demo is saved.** Your own space is never read or changed while the demo is open, and nothing moves between the demo and your space. You can't restore a backup inside the demo.

**Leaving it**

- If you typed something in the demo, **Exit Demo** asks first: **Discard and Exit Demo** or **Keep Editing**.
- If you opened the demo from **Settings**, Exit Demo takes you back to **your own Home**, as it is saved now, with your month and Bills view as you left them. If another tab changed your space in the meantime, you see that latest version.
- If you opened the demo from **Welcome**, Exit Demo takes you back to **Welcome**.

## 5. Getting Started

After **Create My Space**, **Getting Started** appears at the top of Home. It shows one step at a time, in the order below, and a row with the six steps (done, skipped or still to do).

- Each step has a button that opens the form that already exists (for example **Add Income** opens Money › Income with a new income), and **Skip for now** (step 3 also has **I Have None**, step 5 **Not Now**). After a skip, the keyboard goes to the next step's question.
- A step is done when your space has what it asks for. Nothing is filled in for you.
- Step 4 lists the payments your cards and loans already added (**Already in your bills: …**), so you don't add them twice.
- Step 6 ends with **Show Me Add** (it takes you to **+ Add**) and **Done**.
- **Hide this guide** takes it off Home. **Settings › Help & Guide › Show Getting Started on Home** brings it back; skipped steps come back too.
- Your progress is kept on this device, for this space only. It isn't part of your space or its backup. In the demo it starts hidden, lasts only while the demo is open, and Reset Demo or Exit Demo forget it.

Add things in this order, and skip anything that doesn't apply to you. You can come back to any step later.

| Step | Where | What to add | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Money › Income** | Each income you usually receive, such as a salary, with its usual amount and pay day. | Home can then show the income you expect each month. |
| 2 | **Money › Accounts & Savings** | The account you spend from, any cash, savings and investments, each with today's balance. If you don't know a balance, leave it empty. | You enter money you already have only once. Goals use the savings you enter here. |
| 3 | **Money › Cards & Loans › Add Card or Loan** | Each card or loan, with its Remaining Balance and monthly Payment. Keep **Add this payment to my monthly bills** checked. | The Payment is added to your bills for you, so you won't add it twice. |
| 4 | **Bills › Add Bill** | Rent, phone, insurance, subscriptions and anything else you pay regularly. Check the list first: your card and loan payments are already there. | Each payment is entered only once. |
| 5 | **+ Add › Add Goal** (optional) | Something you want to save for, such as a trip. | A goal gives part of your savings a purpose. It doesn't add money. |
| 6 | Every day or week | Add spending, mark bills as paid and record income when it arrives. | Home stays up to date. |

**Avoid counting money twice:**

- Money you already have is entered **once**, as an account balance.
- A new goal starts at 0. To count savings you already have, add them to the goal as **Savings I already have**. Your savings total stays the same.
- A card or loan Payment lives in its bill. Don't add a second bill for the same payment.

## 6. Moving Around

The main tabs are **Home**, **Bills**, **Goals**, **Money** and **History**. Each destination has an icon and a visible name. The floating navigation stays available while you scroll, including in details, editing, Settings and Help. Use Tab, then Enter or Space; Skip to content moves focus to the page.

| Screen width | Where things are |
| --- | --- |
| Phone (narrower than 740 px) | The navigation floats at the bottom. The header has the logo and gear, with **+ Add** on the main screens. Home is one column. |
| Tablet (740 to 1023 px) | The navigation floats at the bottom, with icons beside the labels. The header is the same as on a phone. Home is one column. |
| Computer (1024 px or wider) | The navigation is a compact **floating rail** on the left: logo, five destinations, **Help** and **Settings**. These controls stay in the rail in details too. Home is one column up to 1199 px. |
| Wide computer (1200 px or wider) | As above, and Home shows its areas in **two columns**. |

- In two columns, Home's areas follow the order you chose in **Customize Home**: left to right, then the next row. The keyboard and a screen reader follow the same order. If the number of areas is odd, the last one takes the whole row.
- While **Customize Home** is open, Home goes back to one column, so the list you reorder is the order you read.
- Bill, goal, card and loan details keep **Back** and the main navigation. The highlighted destination is the one you opened the detail from; Back and Forward restore that context. Leaving a changed form still asks before dropping what you typed.
- Motion is decorative. With reduced motion, the active indicator changes instantly. Focus remains visible and labels remain shown.
- Each control is shown once. When the window changes width, the logo and Settings move between the header and the sidebar; they are never in both.

- **Home**: your month at a glance.
- **Bills**: the bills of the month (Calendar or List), and **All Bills** at the end, with every bill whatever the month.
- **Goals**: the **Emergency Fund** and your goals.
- **Money**: **Income**, **Accounts & Savings** and **Cards & Loans**, to look at and change in one place. Each is a block that opens and closes; **Expand All** and **Collapse All** open or close the three. One Add action stays in each section header. The labelled **?** buttons open contextual help and link to Help & Guide; **About Your data** explains local storage or the demo.
- **History**: everything you recorded.
- The logo at the top takes you to Home.
- The gear opens Settings, where **Help & Guide** and **Reports & Backup** are.

**Help & Guide**

**Settings › Help & Guide** (and **Help** in the sidebar on a computer) has a short version of this guide, in chapters that open and close, **Getting Started** and **Your Monthly Check-in**. In the demo it says that Alex's information is invented.

A **?** next to a figure opens a short note under it: two or three sentences, an example from the demo and **Read more in the guide**, which opens Help & Guide on that chapter. They are next to: **Result**, **Expected and Received** and **Planned Bills and Spent** (This Month), **Savings** (Money › Accounts & Savings), **Remaining Balance** (a card or loan), **New savings and Savings I already have** (adding to a goal), **Download Backup and Download Both Copies** and **Hiding amounts** (Settings).

**+ Add**

**+ Add** is in the header, next to the gear, on every tab (Home, Bills, Goals, Money and History). It isn't in the month bar, and there is only one. It opens a short list. First what happened (records), then, after a line, what you plan. Each choice opens a form or place that already exists elsewhere in the app. A choice only shows when it applies (for example **Add Transfer** needs two accounts).

| Choice | Shows when | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add Spending** | always | History, with a new spending form |
| **Record Income Received** | an income source is active this month | Money › Income for this month: with one source, its payment of this month; with more, the note that lists them (see **Where links take you**) |
| **Add Transfer** | you have two or more accounts | History, with a new transfer form |
| **Update a Balance** | you have an account | Money › Accounts & Savings. With one account, it opens on **Update Balance**; with more, you choose the account. |
| **Add a Contribution** | you have an active goal or Emergency Fund | With one, that goal, on the amount. With more, Goals says to choose one, and the goal opens on the amount. |
| **Mark a Bill as Paid** | a bill of this month isn't paid yet | Bills › List, this month, **Pending** bills, on the first **Mark as Paid** |
| **Add Income** | always | Money › Income, with a new income source form |
| **Add Bill** | always | a new bill |
| **Add Goal** | always | New Goal. What you typed is kept while the app stays open. |

**Cancel** on a form that + Add opened, or **Back**, returns to where you used + Add, in the same place, with the keyboard on **+ Add**. After saving, you stay where the form is, and **Back** returns there too. **Mark a Bill as Paid** shows this month in Bills; **Back** returns to the month you were looking at.

If you typed something and leave in another way (a tab, the browser's Back), the app asks first, as described under **Leaving a form you changed** below. Each choice opens its form once: coming back to Money or History later doesn't open a new form again.

Earlier versions had **Cards** and **Data** tabs. Their contents are now in Money, Bills, Goals and Settings: nothing was removed. If you go Back to a screen saved by an earlier version, the app shows Money.

**Choosing a month**

At the top of Home there is a month bar: the arrows go to the month before or after, and the month name opens a list. Home, Bills, **Cards & Loans** (in Money) and the bill and loan details all use the same month. Money's Income and Accounts & Savings don't use it. History has its own period.

**What the app remembers while it stays open:**

- the chosen month,
- how Bills, History and Money look (view, filters, open blocks), and the Goals filter,
- where you were in Bills, History, Money and Goals when you open a bill, a card or loan, a goal or an account from them.

**Back from a detail** goes to where you opened it. A card or loan opened from Money returns to Money, with the same block open, in the same place, on the card or loan you opened. Opened from Home or from a bill, it returns there.

**Back to Home** returns to the same place on Home, with the keyboard on the line or link you used (for example the bill in Left to Pay or the goal in Goals). If that line is no longer there (for example the bill was paid or deleted), it goes to the heading of its part or area. If you had chosen **Show All** in Left to Pay, the full list is still shown.

**Back to Goals and History** works the same way: the same filter (and in History the same period, search, filters, open months and records shown), the same place on the page, and the keyboard on the goal, the Emergency Fund card, **Add Goal** or the record you opened.

History also restores the place and keyboard focus when you tap its tab directly from Money after opening an account from a History record. Bill, goal, card and loan details have both a **Back** button and the main navigation. If you visit another main tab first, that return position is cleared. Opening History then starts at the top, with focus on its heading; its period, search, filters, open months and records shown are still kept.

When you reload the page, these go back to the start.

**Where links take you**

A link opens exactly what it comes from when the app can tell what that is:

| You tap | It opens |
| --- | --- |
| A bill in Left to Pay, Bills or History | That bill, on the month of that payment. |
| A card or loan (Home, Money, a bill) | That card or loan. |
| A goal (Home, Goals, Savings by purpose, History) | That goal. |
| A balance in History | Money › Accounts & Savings, with that account open, or the details of that card or loan. |
| **Income** on Home | Money › Income for the month shown on Home. With one source that month, it opens that source on that month's payment. With more than one, a note names them all and you choose; the app never chooses for you. With none, the note says so. |
| **Spent** on Home | History on that month, with no search or filter. |
| **Planned Bills** on Home | Bills, on the same month. |

Income keeps its own view: opening it for a month doesn't change the month used by Home, Bills and Cards & Loans. If a source of that month has ended (or not started yet), the Income list shows **All** so it can be found. **Close** hides the note.

**Leaving a form you changed**

If you typed something and haven't saved it, the app asks **Leave without saving?** before anything closes it. It lists the forms that would close, and it asks when you:

- tap a tab, the logo, the gear, a **Back** button or a link to another screen,
- use the browser's **Back** or **Forward** (also a long press on Back, or the back swipe on a phone),
- choose another month while an amount for one month is being typed (on Bills, in Spending by Category, or on a bill's screen),
- change the view, filter, search or period so that the form you are using is no longer shown (Bills, Spending by Category, History),
- open another form that takes the place of the one you are using (for example **Edit** on another account).

You can choose:

- **Keep Editing**: nothing changes. You stay on the same screen, with the same month, filters and place on the page, and what you typed is still there.
- **Copy What I Typed**: copies only those forms. The question stays open, so you can still keep editing or leave.
- **Discard and Leave**: closes those forms without saving and goes where you asked, once.

It doesn't ask when:

- nothing was typed, or a form was only opened,
- you tap **Cancel** (that's your own choice to drop it),
- what you typed stays on screen anyway (for example when you close and open a block, or use Expand All or Collapse All in Money or on Home),
- the form is **New Goal** or **Welcome Setup**: these keep what you typed while the app stays open, so you can look elsewhere and come back,
- nothing can be saved because of a problem (see [When Something Goes Wrong](#18-when-something-goes-wrong)): then a form that closes stays in the warning at the top, to copy.

If your last change is still being saved, the app waits for the answer. If it was saved, it moves without asking.

An amount typed for one month is only ever saved for that month. If you choose another month, the app asks first, and with Discard and Leave the amount is dropped before the month changes.

**Current limits:**

- **Reloading, closing the tab or leaving the site**: only the browser can warn you, in its own words, and it can't offer Copy What I Typed. Many phone browsers (Safari on iPhone in particular) often don't warn at all. Everything typed and not saved is lost, including the New Goal and Welcome Setup drafts.
- **Back and Forward pressed several times very quickly**: if the browser's moves arrive in an order the app can't follow, it keeps your form and the question, but **Discard and Leave** stays off and, after a moment, it says "Navigation is taking longer than expected." Keep editing or copy what you typed; to leave, save or cancel the form first, then move.
- When the app can't tell where a Back or Forward move came from (for example the first time after the app was updated), it keeps your form on that step. After **Keep Editing**, one press of Back may seem to do nothing.
- In rare cases, a Back or Forward move that arrives late may change the screen after you left a form. It never closes a form you have changed.
- **The back swipe on iPhone** has been checked on a real iPhone for a form you changed: a swipe you start and then cancel, and a swipe you complete. Coming back from Money has been checked too. The new Home paths (Show All, **+ Add**, coming back to Home) haven't been checked on an iPhone yet, and not every way of moving around has been checked in Safari. Safari may show the previous screen for a moment before the app comes back to your form.
- Home only remembers its place when you come back with **Back**. Opening Home from the tab or the logo starts at the top.
- Money and Goals remember their place only when you come back with **Back**. Opening them from the tab starts at the top. History can also restore its place and record focus when you tap its tab directly from Money after opening an account from a History record, as described above.

## 7. Reading Home

Home has three areas, in this order: **This Month**, **Left to Pay** and **Money & Goals**. The areas are always shown. Inside This Month and Money & Goals there are parts that open and close, and show a short summary when they're closed. At first, every part is closed.

| Area | What it shows | Parts inside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This Month** | The chosen month: Income, Planned Bills, Spent and Result. Its heading shows the month and year. | **Spending by Category**, **Year Summary** |
| **Left to Pay** | The unpaid bills of the chosen month. | (none) |
| **Money & Goals** | Your latest balances, what you owe and your goals. These are **not** this month's figures. | **Accounts & Savings**, **Cards & Loans**, **Goals** |

On a computer, Home is still one column, in the same order as on a phone.

### This Month

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Income** | Income **received** in the month. Until you record what arrived, the income you usually receive shows as **expected**. |
| **Planned Bills** | The bills of the month, **paid or not**. This is what you planned, not what you spent. |
| **Spent** | What you actually spent: paid bills, purchases, and the interest part of loan payments. Each cost counts once. |
| **Result** | Income received minus Spent. **It is not money saved**, and it is not the money in your accounts. |

> **Example (demo, December 2026):** Income 7200,00 zł received. Spent 5097,35 zł. Result 2102,65 zł. Planned Bills were 6401,84 zł for 10 bills, paid or not.

Tap **Income** to open your income in Money, **Planned Bills** to open Bills, and **Spent** to open History.

**How is this worked out?** explains each figure and shows where the result went: into or out of your everyday money, savings and investments, or new debt.

> **Example (demo, August 2026):** the Result was −3037,86 zł. It was covered by 1500,00 zł from Savings, 1160,57 zł from the everyday account and 377,29 zł of new debt.

A line called **Not explained by the records** appears when some records don't say which account they used. See [How Your Records Change the Numbers](#14-how-your-records-change-the-numbers).

### Spending by Category and Year Summary

Both are parts of This Month.

- **Spending by Category**:
  - Choose **Spent** or **Planned**, as a donut or as bars. Tap a category to see what's in it.
  - In Planned, **Change amount** changes a bill for that month only.
  - **Ideas to Review** lists spending you could look at, such as subscriptions. It's a list to review, not advice.
- **Year Summary** is the year of the chosen month, which may not be the current year. Its heading always names that year, for example "2026 · 12 of 12 months so far", and when it's closed it says "Result for 2026: …".
  - Income, spending and result for the year, and the result of each month. Tap a month to open it on Home.
  - Savings and debt at the end of each month.
  - **Reference Notes**: general reference figures, with their source. They aren't limits or advice.

### Left to Pay

The unpaid bills of the chosen month, by due date. Tap a bill to open it, or **Bills** to open the tab. **Mark as Paid** under a bill opens its short form right there (see [Bills and Subscriptions](#10-bills-and-subscriptions)).

The list always says what it counts from:

| Month chosen | What Left to Pay shows |
| --- | --- |
| **This month** | "From today, [date]" (in the demo: "From the demo's date, 31 Dec 2026"). **Overdue**: bills due before today. **Due later in [month]**: due today or later. Then **Coming up · [days]**: the next month's unpaid bills due in the next seven days. Coming up only shows when there are some, and it is never added to the month's total. |
| **A past month** | "These are [month]'s bills. Nothing here is counted from today." **Not marked as paid in [month]**: that month's bills not marked as paid. No Overdue and no Coming up. |
| **A future month** | "These are [month]'s bills. Nothing here is due yet." **Planned for [month]**: that month's bills. No Overdue and no Coming up. |

Unpaid bills of earlier months are never added to the month shown. A bill in Coming up opens on its own month (for example January). **Back** returns to Home on the month you were looking at, on that bill.

Home shows the first five. When there are more than five, **Show All** (with the number of bills) shows the whole list on Home, and **Show Fewer** goes back to five. Show All stays while the app is open, also after you open a bill and come **Back**.

If Show Fewer would hide a payment form you changed, the app asks first. **Keep Editing** keeps the full list and your changes; **Discard and Leave** closes that form and shows five bills.

> **Example (demo, January 2027):** Alex has 11 unpaid bills, so Home shows five and **Show All 11**.

### Money & Goals

The heading says **Latest balances by** a date. That date is the end of the chosen month, or today if the month hasn't ended yet. Each line uses the latest balance you entered up to that date, and shows its own date: it doesn't mean every account was updated that day. A balance you never entered shows as unknown, never as 0.

- **Accounts & Savings**:
  - The last balance of each kind of account, with its date. Debt is never taken away from it.
  - **Savings** shows how your savings are split by purpose.
  - **Edit Balances** opens Accounts & Savings in Money.
- **Cards & Loans**: the last Remaining Balance you entered for each card and loan, added up. Monthly payments aren't added, because they pay the debt back. **Open Cards & Loans** opens it in Money.
- **Goals**:
  - Your goals and how much each one holds. They are part of your Savings, not extra money.
  - It may suggest starting an emergency fund. **Maybe Later** hides the suggestion.
- **Open Goals** (next to the area's name) opens the Goals tab. To add a goal, use **+ Add › Add Goal** in the header.

**Balances are snapshots.** A balance is true on the date you entered it. Home shows the latest balance up to the end of the month you are looking at (or up to today, for the current month). The app never works out past balances from today's balance.

## 8. Income

In **Money › Income**:

- **Add an income source**: name, usual net amount, pay day, first month and, if it ends, last month.
- **Record Received**: open the source and find the month.
  - The amount starts as the usual amount. The date starts as the pay day if it has passed, or today if it hasn't.
  - Change both to what actually arrived. The date can't be after today.
- **Change** edits a payment you recorded. **Not Received** removes it.
- **Remove** takes away the expected income. What already arrived stays in History.
- The filter shows **Active**, **Upcoming**, **Ended** or **All** sources. Active, Upcoming and Ended are worked out for today (the demo's date in the demo).
- Income doesn't use the month chosen in Cards & Loans. Open a source and choose a year to see its payments.

Recording income doesn't change any account balance. See [How Your Records Change the Numbers](#14-how-your-records-change-the-numbers).

## 9. Accounts, Cash and Savings

In **Money › Accounts & Savings**, add each account with its kind: **Daily Spending**, **Savings**, **Investments** or **Other**.

- **Update Balance** records a new balance with its date. The earlier balance stays in History.
- **Delete** can remove an account with no movements, even if it has saved balances (for example, an account created by mistake with an initial balance). It asks first: confirming deletes the account and all its balance updates from History, and cannot be undone. **Cancel** keeps the account and its balances. Other accounts and their records stay.
- Bill payments, income, spending, transfers in either direction and goal movements keep the account from being deleted. The reason appears beside Delete before you try to save. The account and all its history stay; you can update its balance instead. Balances alone do not block deletion.
- If you don't know a balance, leave it empty. It shows as **Not entered**, never as 0.
- **Savings** is what your savings accounts hold. Goals and the Emergency Fund are purposes inside your savings, not extra money:

  **Savings = goals + Emergency Fund + savings with no purpose yet**

> **Example (demo, December 2026):** Savings 2900,00 zł = Trip to Japan 1800,00 + New Bike 200,00 + Emergency Fund 900,00 + 0,00 with no purpose.

**Savings by purpose**, under your accounts in Money, shows the same split as **Accounts & Savings** on Home: each goal, the Emergency Fund and Unassigned. It doesn't add anything to your Savings.

**Savings over time**, under Savings by purpose, shows your savings at the end of each of the last 12 months. A month only has a point if you entered a savings balance in it; a month without one stays empty (**Not entered**), even though Money still shows your last balance. With one point you see the figure instead: update your balance next month to see how it changes. See **Reading the charts** in §14.

Money you don't spend doesn't become savings by itself. Update your balances from your bank to keep them right.

## 10. Bills and Subscriptions

The **Bills** tab shows the bills of the chosen month.

- **Calendar** shows the bills on their due days.
  - Tap a day, then **View day details**, to see its bills.
  - **+ Add Bill** adds a bill on that day.
  - **Back to calendar** returns to the calendar.
- **List** shows every bill with **Mark as Paid** or **✓ Paid** (with the date and the account). See **Mark as Paid** below.
  - **Change Amount for [month]** changes that month only.
  - **Details** opens the bill.
- The **All / Pending / Paid** filter changes the list only. The month's totals stay the same.
- **All Bills**, at the end of Bills, lists every bill with its usual amount, whatever the month above. Its filter shows **Active**, **Upcoming**, **Ended** or **All** bills. Tap a bill to open it; Back returns to the same place.
- **Add Bill**: Bill name, Amount, Amount not known yet, Due day and Frequency. Choose Every month, Every year or One month only (Every month is the default). The bill starts in the selected month. **More details** starts closed and has Category (Other by default), an optional end month for repeating bills, Note and the card or loan it pays. The summary next to Frequency explains when the bill is due. More details summarizes your choices while closed. Amount not known yet is a checkbox just below Amount. Check it to save an unknown amount (never zero); Amount is disabled with one explanation. Uncheck it to enter an amount again; the explanation disappears. Save adds one bill; Cancel drops the draft and Back asks before leaving changes.
- **Edit Details** keeps the existing first and last month, yearly billing, pauses, amounts and payments when you save other details. More details contains the existing date and linking controls. The Frequency selector is for new bills; editing details keeps the bill’s original frequency. Change Usual Amount still asks which month the change starts from; earlier amounts stay as recorded. Subscription pause and cancellation actions remain in the bill detail. To repeat a one-month bill, choose No end month. Skipped months lists months with no charge, including pauses and a cancellation before the first charge.

In a bill's own screen:

- **This month's amount** can be different from the usual amount. **Not Known Yet** leaves it unknown, which is never treated as 0. **Use Usual Amount** goes back to the usual amount.
- **Change Usual Amount** changes it from a month you choose. Earlier months stay the same.
- **Mark as Paid** or **Mark as Not Paid** for the chosen month (see below). A paid month shows **Paid on [date] · [account]** and, for a loan, the interest you entered.
- **Edit Details** and **Delete Bill**.

Changing the first or last month, cancellation or a pause cannot leave out any
additional recorded payments, including advance payments. An old inconsistency
does not block an unrelated safe change. The app does not remove or move payments
to make the change fit. A date error names the paid month and the date to choose.

Payments restored from an older backup stay in History, totals and reports even
if their month is outside the bill's dates or pause. The bill detail explains this.
They count once in Planned Bills (paid or not) and never become unpaid charges.

**Delete Bill** is separate: when payments exist, its confirmation says how many
will be removed from History and reports. Cancel keeps them. A confirmed deletion
removes the bill and its monthly records together; its linked card or loan and
Remaining Balance stay. If another tab changes the data, reopen the confirmation
on the latest version before deleting.

**Mark as Paid**

**Mark as Paid** opens the same short form on Home (Left to Pay), in the Bills list and on the bill. Nothing is marked with a single tap.

| Field | What it suggests | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Amount** | The month's amount. | If you change it, only that month changes. When the amount isn't known yet, you have to enter it. With the amounts hidden, the form doesn't show it and keeps it as it is. |
| **Paid on** | The due date if it has passed, otherwise today. | It must be a real calendar date and can't be after today. |
| **Paid from** | Your account if you have exactly one **Daily Spending** account. Otherwise you choose. | Only accounts: a card isn't an account. **Not sure** saves no account, and then this payment won't match your account balances. |
| **Interest in this payment** | Empty. Shown only for a loan. | Optional, from your statement. Without it, the interest isn't counted in Spent: the app never guesses it. It can't be more than the amount. |

**Save as Paid** saves the amount and the payment together, as one change. Paying never changes a card's or loan's **Remaining Balance**: update it from your statement. There are no partial payments, and no Undo. To correct a payment, use **Mark as Not Paid** and mark it again.

If the payment's month is no longer scheduled for that bill, **Mark as Not Paid**
shows an explanation and keeps the payment. Removing its paid status would make
it inaccessible. To change that status, the month must first be included in the
bill's dates and not skipped. This does not create an unpaid bill automatically.
The payment still counts once in History and reports. Its recorded details can
still be edited while it remains paid.

**Mark as Not Paid** asks first. It removes the date, the account and the interest saved for that month; the month's amount stays.

In the list, **✓ Paid** is announced as **Paid. Mark as Not Paid**, followed by the bill and month. Pressing **Mark as Paid** again asks before closing a form you changed. **Mark as Paid** and **Change Amount** use one editor at a time in the list and bill details: switching asks before discarding changes. **Keep Editing** preserves what you typed; **Cancel** explicitly closes its form and discards that draft.

On Home, a bill you mark as paid leaves Left to Pay, and a message says it was saved. In the Bills list with **Pending** chosen, the bill leaves the list in the same way.

**Manage Subscription** appears near the top of a subscription's details:
- **Status: Active / Upcoming / Ended** uses the same rule here and in All Bills, as of today, independently of the month you are viewing. Upcoming means its first month is still ahead. A plan with an end is Ended when its last scheduled charge is due today or is in the past, even when an older annual plan has a later end month. This does not remove overdue charges or recorded payments.
- **End Subscription** previews the first month without planned charges and the months kept. Known amounts give estimated savings for the displayed charges; unknown amounts are excluded.
- **Confirm End Subscription** records the ending here. **Keep Subscription** leaves everything as it was and returns the keyboard focus to End Subscription. The app does not cancel the service with your provider.
- Recorded payments stay in History and reports. If ending would exclude a recorded payment, the app explains the paid month and blocks confirmation.
- Ending before the first charge shows **Ended**, with no invented charge. Existing overdue charges remain.
- **Review Subscription** contains your Needed / Could adjust / Optional rating, provider options and the existing **What If I Pause It?** simulation. Its **Cancel** returns focus to What If I Pause It?. This finite skip is not a full Pause/Resume service. If a provider preference blocks confirmation, change it in Review Subscription.

## 11. Cards and Loans

In **Money › Cards & Loans** you see each card and loan with its **Remaining Balance**, the date of that balance, and the payment of the chosen month. The month bar inside the block is the same month as Home and Bills. At the top, **Remaining Balance** adds up what you still owe, and **Monthly Payments** adds up the payments of that month. Home's **Open Cards & Loans** opens this block.

**Add Card or Loan:**

- kind, name and yearly interest,
- due day; for a card, also the statement closing day and the minimum payment,
- for a loan, the first and last payment,
- the monthly **Payment**,
- the Remaining Balance and its date.

Keep **Add this payment to my monthly bills** checked. The Payment then shows in Bills and Left to Pay, and it is counted only once. If you uncheck it, the Payment is kept on the card or loan and doesn't appear in Bills.

**In a card or loan:**

- **Update Balance**: enter the balance from your bank or statement, with the date it was true. The earlier balance stays in **Balance History**.
- **Paying the bill never changes the Remaining Balance.** Update it from your statement.
- **Estimate** shows about how many months it would take to pay off at the usual Payment, with the same interest. **What If I Pay Extra?** compares it with a higher payment. Both are calculations only, and your bank may calculate differently.
- **Remaining Balance over time**: the last balance you entered in each of the last 12 months, as a solid line, and the **Estimate** as a dashed line labelled "Estimate if you pay … a month" (up to 12 months ahead). The estimate starts from your latest balance and only shows when that balance is from this month or last month. Choose a point and **Show in Balance History** takes you to that balance.
- **Edit Details** and **Delete**. If a bill pays this card or loan, you choose whether to delete the bill too or keep it.

> **Example (demo, 31 December 2026):** Cards & Loans on Home: 12 266,98 zł Remaining Balance = 0,00 + 2644,35 + 9622,63.

## 12. Spending and Transfers

In **History**:

- **Add Spending**: date, amount, category, a description (optional), **Paid from** (an account or a card) and **How often**: Usual spending, Once a year, or One-off (unusual).
  - A purchase with a card counts as spending on the day you buy it. It becomes card debt until you pay the card, and paying the card isn't counted as spending again.
- **Add Transfer** (when you have two or more accounts): money moved between your own accounts. It isn't income or spending.
- Open a record to change or delete it.

## 13. My Goals

A goal gives part of your savings a purpose. **Creating goals is optional.**

The **Goals** tab shows:

- the **Emergency Fund** on its own card (or **Start an Emergency Fund** if you don't have one). It isn't added to **Saved in Goals**;
- **Saved in Goals** and **Total Targets**, for your goals only;
- your goals, with the filter **All**, **Flexible** or **With Date**. The filter stays while the app is open.

Both your goals and the Emergency Fund are part of your Savings, not extra money.

**Add Goal** (from the Goals tab, or **+ Add › Add Goal** in the header):

- A **name** is required.
- A **target amount** is optional. Without a target, the goal shows how much it holds, with no percentage.
- A **date** is optional, and only possible with a target. A goal with a date is **With Date**. One without a date is **Flexible**.
- **Review Goal**, then **Create Goal**. A new goal starts at 0. No monthly amount is needed.
- **Back** (at the top) and **Cancel** return to the screen you came from, for example Home or Goals. What you typed stays while the app is open, so you can come back to it.

**Add or Take Out** (in the goal), with **Add** or **Withdraw**. Choose where the money comes from or where it goes:

| Choice | What happens | Your savings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New savings** | Money you saved now. The goal and your savings account both go up, once. | Go up |
| **Savings I already have** | Savings that had no purpose now belong to the goal. | Stay the same |
| **Back to unassigned savings** | The money stays in savings, with no purpose. | Stay the same |
| **Out of my savings** | You spent it or moved it out of savings. The goal and your savings account both go down. | Go down |

- **New savings** and **Out of my savings** need a savings account with a balance entered, and a date on or after that balance's date.
- You can't take out more than a goal holds, or give a goal more savings than have no purpose.

**Pace:** for a goal with a date, the app shows how much you'd need to save each month. It counts whole months, starting a month from today, and rounds up. This is what it would take, not what you can afford.

**Explore:**

- **If I save each month** shows about how many months it would take, and the date.
- **Or reach it by** a date shows the monthly amount needed.
- Nothing changes unless you choose **Use This Date** and confirm.

**Emergency Fund:**

- **Review My Emergency Fund** adds up your essential costs per month.
- It suggests a target for 1, 3 or 6 months, or another number of months from 0.5 to 24.
- You decide whether to save it.

**Saved over time** shows what the goal held after each record: what was already saved when you added it, then a step up for each Contribution and a step down for each Withdrawal. With a target, a dotted line marks it; with a date, a line marks the date. Choose a step, then **Show in Savings Activity** to see that record. With no record or only one, you see the figure or **Add Contribution** instead.

**After saving.** Only once the change is saved, the message says what it did: "Added 200,00 zł to Trip to Japan (new savings)." or "Took out 200,00 zł (back to unassigned savings). Trip to Japan now has 1600,00 zł." The goal's bar moves to the new amount in about 300 ms (it goes down at the same speed, with no red). When a Contribution reaches the target, **Goal reached** shows once. With **Reduce motion** on your device, nothing moves: the amount changes at once and the message stays. In Left to Pay, a bill marked as paid leaves the list and a short message with a check takes its place.

**Change Name, Target or Date** edits a goal. **Delete Goal** removes it: what it held goes back to savings with no purpose, and no balance changes.

## 14. How Your Records Change the Numbers

Each record changes some figures and not others. **Account and loan balances only change when you enter a new balance**, or with a goal's New savings or Out of my savings.

| You record… | Income / Spent / Result | Balances shown | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Income received** | Adds to Income and Result for its month. | No change. | The app doesn't ask which account it went into. |
| **A paid bill** | Adds to Spent, unless it pays a card or loan. | No change. | It records today's date, and no account. |
| **A paid loan bill** | Only its interest is spending, when the interest is known. | No change to the Remaining Balance. | When the interest isn't known, nothing from that payment goes into Spent. |
| **A paid card bill** | Not spending again: the purchases already counted. | No change to the Remaining Balance. | |
| **Spending** | Adds to Spent on its date. | No change. | It records which account or card you used. |
| **A transfer** | Not income or spending. | No change. | It records both accounts. |
| **New balance** | No change. | The new balance shows from its date. | Earlier balances stay in History. |
| **Goal: New savings** | No change. | The savings account goes up. | It adds a new balance on the movement's date. |
| **Goal: Out of my savings** | No change. | The savings account goes down. | It adds a new balance on the movement's date. |
| **Goal: Savings I already have / Back to unassigned** | No change. | No change. | Only the purpose of the money changes. |

**Why "Not explained by the records" can appear:** "How is this worked out?" only follows records that say which account they used. **Mark as Paid** records the account (unless you choose **Not sure**), but the app doesn't record an account for income you receive yet, nor for bills marked as paid before this version. Those amounts can show as **Not explained by the records**. Your Result is still right; only the "where did it go" lines are incomplete.

### Reading the charts

Four charts show change over time: **Income & Spending** (History),
**Savings over time** (Money), **Saved over time** (goals) and
**Remaining Balance over time** (cards and loans).

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Solid bar or line | What you recorded. |
| Outlined bar | Income still expected this month or later. |
| Dashed line labelled Estimate | A calculation, not a record. |
| Not entered or Nothing recorded | A gap; the app does not invent values. |
| A figure instead of a line | Fewer than two records. |

Select a point to read it. Tab reaches the chart; arrows, Home and End move
between records. **Show as Table** shows the same figures. Charts do not animate.
With Hide All Amounts, History hides its chart and table. Money, goals and card
details currently show amounts and their charts, as before.
The help notes on This Month also hide their numeric examples while that area's amounts are hidden.

## 15. History

History lists everything you recorded, grouped by month and day, newest first.

- **Year Overview**: received income (solid), expected income (outlined, this month or later) and spending. Months with no records say Nothing recorded. Choose a month for its list and figures; arrows move between months, and Show as Table offers the same figures.
- **Search**, and **More Filters** for the type of record, category and account. Each filter shows as a label you can remove. **Clear Filters** keeps the period.
- **Show More** loads more records in a month. **Expand All** and **Collapse All** open or close the months.
- A balance in History has the accessible action **Open account** for an account in Money › Accounts & Savings, or **Open card or loan** for a card or loan's details. **Back** returns to the same place with focus on that record. Tapping the History tab directly from Money after opening an account also restores the place and focus; visiting another main tab first clears that return position.

## 16. Hiding Amounts and Customizing Home

**On Home**, the month bar has two buttons next to the month. On a narrow phone they move to a second line.

- **The eye** hides or shows every amount on Home. Its name is **Hide All Amounts** or **Show All Amounts**, and a short message says "Amounts hidden" or "Amounts visible". When everything is hidden, the eye is crossed out.
- **Home options** (the ⋯ button) has:
  - **Expand All** and **Collapse All**, to open or close every part of every area.
  - **Customize Home**: drag an area by its handle, or use **Move up** and **Move down**. **Reset Layout** goes back to This Month, Left to Pay, then Money & Goals, with every part closed. It doesn't show or hide any amount. **Done** finishes.

Each area also has its own eye (for example **Hide amounts in Left to Pay**), to hide the amounts of that area and of every part inside it, including the short summary a part shows when it's closed.

**Bills** and **History** have a **Hide All Amounts** button. They hide their amounts only when all three Home areas are hidden. In Bills this also covers **All Bills**.

These are screen preferences for this device. They don't change any amount, and they aren't in your backup.

**If you used an earlier version** (with seven blocks):

- An area starts hidden if **any** of its old blocks was hidden. For example, if only Year Summary was hidden, the whole This Month area starts hidden. Nothing that was hidden becomes visible.
- The areas keep the order of their first old block, and a part starts open if its old block was open.
- The demo never reads or keeps these preferences.

**Charts and details with hidden amounts.** A chart can show amounts even without numbers, so while amounts are hidden:

- Each chart (the donuts, the bars in Spending by Category and in History, Year Summary's months) is replaced by a box that says **Chart hidden with the amounts**, with **Show Amounts**. On Home, Show Amounts shows that area again; in Bills and History, it shows every amount.
- Nothing that comes from the amounts shows: no bar lengths, no red or green, no warning colour on a result, no percentages, no progress bars, no "Spent more than received", no sentence naming the months with a deficit, no "Goal reached" or "Target reached", no emergency fund cover, and no "Where the result went".
- Year Summary still lists the months, to open them on Home, all in the same way.
- Lists keep an order that doesn't depend on the amounts: categories in their usual order, Ideas to Review by name. **Unassigned** savings are always listed, so you can't tell whether they are 0.
- When you change a month's amount in Spending by Category › Planned, the field starts empty instead of showing the hidden amount.

**Current limits of hiding amounts:**

- Hiding isn't global. Amounts are **not** hidden in Money (Income, Accounts & Savings, Cards & Loans), Goals, a goal, a bill, a card or loan, or Settings, even when every Home area is hidden.
- Some facts that aren't amounts still show: which bills are paid or overdue, how many bills there are, whether a balance or a bill amount is missing, and the warning when your goals hold more than your savings.
- The PDF report has its own **Include amounts** choice. Hiding amounts on screen doesn't change it.

These are in **Settings › Reports & Backup**.

| | **Export Report** | **Download Backup** | **Download Both Copies** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| What it's for | Reading, printing or sharing. It isn't a backup or a bank statement. | Keeping a copy and bringing it back with **Restore Backup**. | Rescuing information before you erase it or use an older copy. **Restore Backup can't use it.** |
| Where | Settings › Reports & Backup | Settings › Reports & Backup (also in the demo) | The Your Saved Information screen, and Settings › Reports & Backup when the app can only show your space |

- **Export Report:** choose **One Month** or **Whole Year**. With **Include amounts** unchecked, every amount becomes •••• and the report says so on its first page. It also leaves out the charts, the shares, how each deficit was covered and the emergency fund's cover, and lists categories by name.
- **Restore Backup:**
  - Choose a backup file. The app checks it first, and nothing changes until you confirm.

## 18. When Something Goes Wrong

**Another tab or window**

- **Another tab saved a change** and you hadn't typed anything: this tab updates and says "Updated with changes from another tab."
- **Another tab saved a change while you were typing:** what you typed stays on screen and isn't saved. Use **Copy What I Typed**, then **Open Latest Version**. The app asks before it discards what you typed.
- **Another tab erased your space:** if nothing was typed here, you go to Welcome with a message. If something was typed, it stays on screen until you choose **Discard and Close**.

**Saving**

- When a change can't be saved, the form keeps what you typed and tells you why. Try again.
- **The app can only show your space** (for example, if this browser can't save safely): you can look at everything, **Download Backup** and **Download Both Copies**, and **Try Again**, but nothing can be saved.

**Your Saved Information**

This screen appears when the saved information can't be opened. What it offers depends on the problem:

- **The information is damaged, missing, or two saved copies don't match:**
  - **Download Both Copies**: do this first.
  - **Use Older Copy**: only when an older copy can be read. It opens the copy that earlier versions of the app saved, which may not have your latest changes. The copy that can't be opened is kept aside, not deleted.
  - **Erase Saved Information**: removes everything this app saved on this device, so you can start again.
  - **Try Again**.
- **A newer version of the app saved it:** only **Download Both Copies** and **Try Again**. Nothing is replaced or erased. Reload the page or use the app where you used it last.
- **Explore the App** opens the demo from this screen too.

Every option that changes or removes something asks first.

**Erase All Data on This Device** (Settings › Privacy & Security) deletes your whole space after you confirm with **Erase Everything**. This can't be undone.

## 19. Your Monthly Check-in

This is a suggested routine, not a reminder. The app doesn't send notifications.

1. **When income arrives:** Money › Income › Record Received.
2. **As you pay:** mark bills as paid in Bills. Add spending in History, for example once a week.
3. **Once a month:** update your account balances, and the Remaining Balance of each card and loan from your statements.
4. **If you set money aside for a goal:** add it to the goal with **Add or Take Out**.
5. **At the end of the month:** look at This Month and "How is this worked out?", check that Left to Pay is empty, then **Download Backup**.

**+ Add** in the header records spending, income received, transfers, balances, Contributions and bill payments, and adds income, bills and goals, from any tab.
